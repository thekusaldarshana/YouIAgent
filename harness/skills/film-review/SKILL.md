# Render review

Read `film-output/film-plan.md`, the render report, and `film-output/review/contact-sheet.jpg`. Inspect the contact sheet as an image; also watch the MP4 at normal speed and listen if your environment supports it. If either inspection is unavailable, say exactly what was not inspected. Never infer visual quality from source code or a successful HyperFrames check alone.

Write `film-output/visual-review.md` with:

1. **Inspected:** paths, frames or video actually viewed, and whether sound was heard.
2. **What the film shows:** one concrete sentence per sampled frame or scene, noting the focal subject and product proof.
3. **Findings:** specific timestamps for unreadable proof, slide-like held layouts, corner chrome, generic cards, unexplained fades, empty holds, cramped hierarchy, or an overlong ending. Separate observed defects from taste preferences. Do not flag faithful product UI merely because it contains a card or label.
4. **Verdict:** `needs repair`, `reviewed with notes`, or `unreviewed`, with reasons. A scripted check passing is not a taste verdict.
5. **Next edit:** the smallest changes most likely to improve the film, including any issue that needs a human decision.

Use the contact sheet to locate issues, then verify timing and readability against the MP4 when possible. Do not claim to have watched or heard anything you could not access. Do not alter the finished film in this stage.
