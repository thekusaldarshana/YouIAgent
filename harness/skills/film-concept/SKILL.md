# Concept and story

Start with one true product action and its visible result. Write a one-line audience, goal and intended feeling. Explore at least three ideas of different kinds, such as a real task shown end to end, a demonstration centered on the output, or a typographic argument grounded in the product's language. Reject the first generic association with the name or category. Choose one idea and give a brief reason; record the alternatives in `film-plan.md` so the decision can be reviewed.

For each beat, write what belief it buys, what the viewer sees with all text hidden, which source supports it, and how it enters or hands off. If the picture cannot show the claim, change the claim or the beat. Give the actual product proof more time than setup or decoration. A hook, reveal, consequence and ending are useful possibilities, not a fixed template. Find the shortest runtime that makes the real flow readable within the active profile.

Use brand language, not interchangeable slogans or triplets of one-word sentences. Avoid repeated feature cards, title slides, invented stats, and a logo-tagline-button ending by default. These are review prompts, not bans on a faithful view of the product. Plan sound to serve changes in the story. For original music, describe its motif, section changes, accents and resolution; avoid a generic loop.
