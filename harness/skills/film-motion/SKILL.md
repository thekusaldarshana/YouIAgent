# Motion and composition

Read the approved plan, `MOTION_PROFILE.md`, brand evidence, and prepared HyperFrames runtime notes. Design one visual grammar for this film: type, surfaces, camera, transitions, rhythm, and meaning of accent colors. Build the hardest proof moment first in the source, then connect the remaining beats. Use local frozen assets. Keep the composition seek-safe and follow the current HyperFrames contract.

Make product proof readable at normal speed. Favor a meaningful UI fragment, action, and response over a tiny full-page screenshot or a generic UI card. Let important actions cause the next visual state. A cut, carry, mask, camera move, or held breath should be chosen for the idea. Avoid using opacity fades, corner labels, counters, generic gradients, and identical left-headline/right-card layouts as automatic scene solutions. These may be correct when they faithfully show the product or suit the brand; record the reason in the plan or review.

Use the active profile's motion language. The `motion-studio` profile asks for one continuous 2D take and has explicit beat constraints; do not force hard cuts or 3D into it. Do not treat motion on every frame or any measured ease from another film corpus as a universal target. A still moment can be deliberate when it improves comprehension or emphasis. Use `film-motion/mechanics.js` only when a helper serves the story; do not choose the story to fit a helper.
